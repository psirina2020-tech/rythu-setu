Bridging the Guidance Gap for Farmers

Farmers lose significant yield due to delayed or complex technical advice. Rythu Setu provides a voice-first, regional language bridge to trusted agricultural decisions via WhatsApp.

About the Project

Rythu Setu was born within our Innovation Lab to address a critical friction point: the "Information-Action" gap in Indian agriculture. While information exists, it is rarely accessible in the voice-first, local language format that farmers prefer during urgent decision-making moments.

How It Works

Ask

Farmer sends a voice note on WhatsApp describing a crop issue in their native tongue.

Analyze

System assesses the urgency and provides timing guidance or risk warnings.

Escalate

Complex or high-risk queries are routed to human experts for verified intervention.

MVP Scope Analysis

What We Are Solving

  • Reducing decision confusion during pest outbreaks.
  • Providing timing guidance (When to spray/irrigate).
  • Low-literacy accessible voice support.

Not Solving (Future)

  • Exact chemical brand/dosage prescriptions.
  • End-to-end farm automation or IoT.
  • E-commerce or fertilizer sales.
